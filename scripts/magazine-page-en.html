Shakshuka with feta

Eggs poached in a spicy sauce of tomato and red pepper. On the table in half an hour, eaten straight from the pan with warm flatbread for dipping.

Serves 230 minutesEasy

Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 1 red pepper
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 400 g chopped tomatoes
  • 4 eggs
  • 100 g feta
  • fresh parsley to serve
  • flatbread to serve, optional
Tip Like it hot? Add half a finely chopped red chilli with the onion, or a spoonful of harissa with the tomatoes.

Method

  1. Finely chop the onion, slice the pepper into strips and chop the garlic. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan and soften the onion and pepper for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic, cumin and paprika and fry for 1 minute. Pour in the chopped tomatoes, season with salt and pepper and simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  3. Make four wells in the sauce with a spoon and crack an egg into each. Cover the pan and cook the eggs for 6 minutes until the whites are set and the yolks still soft.
  4. Crumble the feta over the top, scatter with parsley and serve with warm flatbread.
